**High Court of Kerala** has announced **Office Attendant Recruitment No. 21/2026** for **17 anticipated vacancies**.

This recruitment is relevant to the Deaf and Hard of Hearing community because **Deaf and Hard of Hearing is explicitly listed as a suitable benchmark disability category** for the Office Attendant post under the **4% reservation for eligible differently abled candidates**.

The notification does not separately state how many of the 17 anticipated vacancies will specifically fall to Deaf/HH candidates, so DeafJobsIndia does **not** claim a fixed number of D/HH vacancies.

## Deaf and Hard of Hearing eligibility confirmed

The notification identifies the following benchmark disability category as suitable for the Office Attendant post:

**Category B — Deaf and Hard of Hearing**

This means Deaf/HH eligibility is based on the post's stated disability suitability and is **not inferred from a generic PwBD provision**.

The recruitment provides **4% reservation for eligible differently abled candidates** according to the applicable Government order.

## Important vacancy clarification

There are **17 anticipated Office Attendant vacancies**.

However, the notification does not give a separate number exclusively reserved for Deaf or Hard of Hearing candidates. Therefore, candidates should not interpret the 4% provision as a guarantee that a specific number of the currently anticipated vacancies will be allotted to D/HH.

## Qualification

Candidates should:

- Have passed **SSLC or an equivalent qualification**
- **Not have acquired graduation** by the closing date of application

Candidates claiming an equivalent qualification should follow the proof requirements in the official notification.

## Age relaxation for Deaf/HH candidates

The recruitment notification provides age relaxation for eligible candidates with benchmark disabilities. Deaf and Hard of Hearing candidates should check the official age provisions and certificate requirements before applying.

## Application fee

The general application fee is **₹500**.

SC/ST candidates and eligible unemployed differently abled candidates are exempt from the application fee, subject to the notification conditions.

## Selection process

Selection will be based on:

1. **Written Test**
2. **Interview**

The written examination is an objective test covering General Knowledge & Current Affairs, Numerical Ability, Mental Ability and General English.

## Important dates

- **Online application starts:** 23 September 2026
- **Last date for online application:** 26 October 2026
- **Offline fee remittance starts:** 30 October 2026
- **Last date for offline fee remittance:** 6 November 2026

## How to apply

Applications must be submitted through the **official High Court of Kerala recruitment portal**.

Candidates should complete One Time Registration, fill their profile, choose the Office Attendant recruitment and submit the application according to the portal instructions.

## Important note for Deaf and Hard of Hearing candidates

Before applying, check:

- Benchmark disability certificate requirements
- Age and relaxation provisions
- Qualification requirements
- Reservation conditions
- Examination instructions
- Any corrigendum or update issued by the High Court
