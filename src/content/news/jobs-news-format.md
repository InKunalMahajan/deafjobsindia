This sample article demonstrates the future **Jobs News** format on DeafJobsIndia.

## Clear information first

A real recruitment story can summarise the employer or organisation, role, eligibility, location, important dates and application information before providing additional context.

## Source-first publishing

When real job news is published, the final article should link to the official employer, recruitment notice or application source whenever possible.

This keeps job reporting useful while preserving the news-style identity of DeafJobsIndia.
