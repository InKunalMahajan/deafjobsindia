**Hindustan Copper Limited (HCL)**, a Government of India enterprise under the Ministry of Mines, has announced recruitment for **Fixed Term Executive I–VI** posts under **Advertisement No. Estt./1/2036/2026-27**.

This recruitment is important for the Hard of Hearing community because the official notification includes **1 post specifically reserved for HH (Hard of Hearing) candidates**.

The notification also identifies **Geology, Electrical, Mechanical, Civil, Human Resources and Finance** among disciplines suitable for HH candidates. This is not an assumption based only on general PwBD eligibility.

## Hard of Hearing reservation confirmed

The official HCL recruitment notification explicitly provides **1 reserved vacancy for HH candidates**.

For PwBD reservation, candidates must satisfy the benchmark disability requirements stated in the notification. The relevant benchmark is generally **not less than 40% disability**, supported by the prescribed disability certificate.

Therefore, DeafJobsIndia classifies this as a **confirmed Hard of Hearing recruitment opportunity**.

## Disciplines identified for HH candidates

The official notification identifies HH suitability for relevant posts in these disciplines:

- Geology
- Electrical
- Mechanical
- Civil
- Human Resources
- Finance

Candidates should check the detailed vacancy table because qualification, experience, executive level and other conditions differ by discipline and post.

## Total vacancies

HCL has advertised **43 Fixed Term Executive vacancies** across different disciplines and executive levels.

Selected candidates may be posted at **Hindustan Copper Limited projects, units or offices anywhere in India**, depending on organizational requirements.

Selection is primarily through a **Personal Interview**, subject to the detailed conditions and shortlisting rules in the official advertisement.
