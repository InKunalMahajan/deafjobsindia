**Brahmaputra Cracker and Polymer Limited (BCPL)** has announced **Advertisement No. BCPL-NE/06/2026** for recruitment to non-executive posts.

This recruitment is especially important for the Deaf and Hard of Hearing community because the official vacancy table includes **1 Operator (Chemical) Trainee post reserved for PwBD Category B and specifically identifies Deaf (D) and Hard of Hearing (HH) candidates as suitable**.

This is a **direct D/HH vacancy** and not an assumption based on general PwBD eligibility.

## Deaf / Hard of Hearing vacancy confirmed

The PwBD vacancy table in the advertisement shows:

- **Post:** Operator (Chemical) Trainee
- **Grade:** S-3
- **PwBD Category B:** 1 vacancy
- **Post identified as suitable for:** **D, HH**

The notification defines:

- **D = Deaf**
- **HH = Hard of Hearing**

Therefore, DeafJobsIndia classifies this as a **confirmed Deaf / Hard of Hearing recruitment opportunity**.

## Overall vacancy position

BCPL has advertised **21 regular non-executive vacancies** across posts such as:

- Foreman (Chemical)
- Foreman (Civil)
- Junior Superintendent (Human Resources)
- Foreman (Instrumentation)
- Operator (Chemical)
- Technician (Electrical)
- Technician (Instrumentation)
- Technician (Mechanical)

In addition, there are **3 posts reserved for PwBD candidates**:

| PwBD Post | Vacancies | Identified Disability Category |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Operator (Chemical) Trainee | 1 | **Deaf (D), Hard of Hearing (HH)** |
| Technician (Mechanical) Trainee | 1 | ASD (M), ID, SLD, MI and specified combinations |
| Accounts Assistant Trainee | 1 | Blind (B), Low Vision (LV) |

This gives **24 vacancies overall: 21 regular posts + 3 PwBD trainee posts**.

## Qualification for Operator (Chemical) Trainee

Candidates must have either:

- **B.Sc. with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics** with minimum **50% marks**, or
- **B.Sc. (Honours) in Chemistry** with minimum **50% marks**.

The advertisement provides relaxation in the minimum qualifying percentage for PwBD candidates where applicable. Candidates should read the full notification carefully before applying.

## Training and stipend

Selected candidates for the Operator (Chemical) Trainee post will undergo a **minimum one-year training period**.

The monthly stipend is:

**₹21,000 per month**

## Age limit and PwBD relaxation

The upper age limit shown for the Operator (Chemical) Trainee post is **30 years**.

The advertisement provides PwBD age relaxation of:

- **10 years** for PwBD General / EWS
- **13 years** for PwBD OBC (NCL)
- **15 years** for PwBD SC / ST

The maximum upper age after all possible relaxations cannot exceed **56 years**.

## Benchmark disability requirement

To claim PwBD reservation or concessions, candidates must have **not less than 40% of the relevant disability** and must submit a valid disability certificate in the prescribed Government of India format.

## Application fee

According to the advertisement:

- **General / EWS / OBC (NCL):** ₹200
- **SC / ST / PwBD:** Exempt from application fee, subject to valid supporting certificates

## Selection process

The selection process may include one or more of the following depending on BCPL requirements:

- Skill Test
- Proficiency Test
- Endurance Test
- Written Test

BCPL will communicate the exact process to eligible candidates.

## How to apply

Applications must be submitted **online through the BCPL website**. No other mode of application is accepted.

Candidates should keep ready:

- Valid email ID
- Recent passport-size photograph
- Signature image
- Proof of age
- Benchmark Disability certificate
- Educational qualification certificates
- Other applicable category certificates

A hard copy of the application is **not required to be sent to BCPL** after online submission.

## Important dates

- **Online application starts:** 10 September 2026 at 8:00 AM
- **Last date to apply:** 9 October 2026 at 5:00 PM

## Important note for Deaf and Hard of Hearing candidates

The official advertisement specifically identifies the Operator (Chemical) Trainee vacancy for **Deaf (D) and Hard of Hearing (HH)** candidates under PwBD Category B.

Candidates should still verify their qualification, age, benchmark disability certificate, medical fitness requirements and all other conditions in the full BCPL advertisement before submitting the application.
