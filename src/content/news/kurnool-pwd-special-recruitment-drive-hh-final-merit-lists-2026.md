The **Kurnool District Administration, Government of Andhra Pradesh** has published final merit and rejected lists under its **Special Recruitment Drive for Persons with Disabilities (PwDs)**.

The official recruitment page includes result lists for **HH-category candidates**, making this update relevant to Deaf and Hard of Hearing job seekers who participated in the recruitment process.

## HH-category result lists published

The official Kurnool recruitment page lists final merit or rejected lists for posts including:

- **Junior Assistant — HH General**
- **Junior Office Assistant (Typist) — HH General**
- **LD Computer — HH General**
- **ANM Grade III — HH Women** rejected list

The recruitment page also includes lists for other PwD categories such as VH and OH.

## What candidates should do

Candidates who applied under the Special Recruitment Drive should open the official Kurnool District recruitment page and check the relevant merit or rejected-list file for their post and disability category.

Use your application details carefully while checking the published lists. If your name or application status requires clarification, follow the instructions provided by the district administration in the official recruitment notice.

## Important note

This is a **result/update notice**, not a new application invitation. DeafJobsIndia is publishing it because the official district recruitment page specifically includes HH-category merit and rejected lists under a PwD recruitment drive.

Always rely on the official district website for the final result documents and any further appointment or verification instructions.
