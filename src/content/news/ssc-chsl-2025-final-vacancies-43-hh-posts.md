The **Staff Selection Commission (SSC)** has published the **final vacancy position for the Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination-2025**, with the vacancy statement dated **18 June 2026**.

The final list shows **3,522 total vacancies**, including **43 vacancies under the HH (Hard of Hearing) category**.

## Final PwD vacancy totals

According to the official SSC vacancy table:

- **Total vacancies:** 3,522
- **OH:** 35
- **HH:** 43
- **VH:** 39
- **Other PwD:** 37

For DeafJobsIndia readers, the key figure is the **43 HH vacancies** shown in the final CHSL 2025 vacancy statement.

## Important clarification

This article is about **SSC CHSL 2025 final vacancies**. The figure of **43 HH vacancies must not be treated as the vacancy count for SSC CHSL 2026**.

Candidates should use the official SSC website and the relevant examination notice for eligibility, disability suitability, examination stages and appointment conditions.
