This is **demo editorial content** included with the Step 3 theme. It shows how a real DeafJobsIndia article will look once you begin publishing verified news.

## What this newsroom is designed to cover

DeafJobsIndia can publish updates across jobs, education, government schemes, training, technology, career guidance, events and Deaf community news.

The goal of the custom Astro theme is to keep every story easy to scan, easy to navigate and consistent across desktop and mobile screens.

## How future stories will work

Each article is stored as a Markdown file with structured information such as the title, category, publication date, author, image and tags. Astro then automatically creates the article page and places the story in the correct sections of the website.

This means the design and the content stay separate, making the site easier to maintain as the newsroom grows.
