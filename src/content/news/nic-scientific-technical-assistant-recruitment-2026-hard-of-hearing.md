The **National Informatics Centre (NIC)**, under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Government of India, has invited online applications for **Scientific/Technical Assistant-A** posts under **Advertisement No. NIC/STA/2026/2**.

A total of **376 vacancies** have been announced across Computer Sciences & Information Technology, Electronics & Communication Engineering, and Data Science & Artificial Intelligence.

This recruitment is especially relevant for **Hard of Hearing (HH)** candidates because the official vacancy table includes **6 HH-reserved vacancies** under horizontal PwBD reservation.

## Quick recruitment details

| Detail | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Organization | National Informatics Centre (NIC) |
| Post | Scientific/Technical Assistant-A |
| Advertisement No. | NIC/STA/2026/2 |
| Total vacancies | 376 |
| HH vacancies | 6 |
| Pay level | Level 6 — ₹35,400 to ₹1,12,400 |
| Application start | 1 September 2026, 10:00 AM |
| Last date | 30 September 2026, 5:30 PM |
| Selection | Valid GATE 2024 / 2025 / 2026 score + document verification |
| Application mode | Online |
| Location | All India |

## Vacancy breakdown

| Discipline | Total Posts | HH Vacancies |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Computer Sciences & Information Technology | 300 | 4 |
| Electronics & Communication Engineering | 26 | 1 |
| Data Science & Artificial Intelligence | 50 | 1 |
| **Total** | **376** | **6** |

The PwBD reservation is **horizontal**, meaning these reservations operate within the overall category-wise vacancy totals.

## Important note for Deaf and Hard of Hearing candidates

The official vacancy table specifically lists **Hard of Hearing (HH)** vacancies.

DeafJobsIndia is therefore describing this recruitment as **HH-reserved** and is **not claiming a separate Deaf (D) vacancy** unless NIC publishes an update or clarification explicitly including Deaf candidates.

Candidates should read the full official notification and confirm their benchmark disability category and eligibility before applying.

## Educational qualification and GATE requirement

Candidates must have the prescribed qualification in a relevant discipline, such as **M.Sc., MS, MCA, B.E. or B.Tech**, as specified in the official notification.

A valid **GATE 2024, GATE 2025 or GATE 2026** score is required in the corresponding paper for the discipline applied for.

The recruitment covers the following streams:

- Computer Sciences & Information Technology
- Electronics & Communication Engineering
- Data Science & Artificial Intelligence

## Selection process

Selection is based on the candidate's valid **GATE score**, followed by **document verification** for shortlisted candidates.

Candidates should check the official notification for the exact GATE paper code, qualifying degree and selection conditions for each discipline.

## Salary

The Scientific/Technical Assistant-A post is in **Pay Level 6** of the 7th Central Pay Commission:

**₹35,400 – ₹1,12,400**

Allowances will apply according to Government of India rules.

## Application fee

According to the recruitment details:

- **SC / ST / PwBD / Women:** No application fee
- **UR / OBC (NCL) / EWS:** ₹800 per discipline

Candidates applying for more than one discipline should carefully check the official fee instructions.

## Important dates

- **Online application opens:** 1 September 2026 at 10:00 AM
- **Last date to apply:** 30 September 2026 at 5:30 PM
- **Eligibility cut-off date:** 30 September 2026
- **Document verification:** To be announced by NIC

## Before applying

Please check the official advertisement for:

- Exact educational qualification
- Correct GATE paper and valid GATE year
- HH / PwBD reservation conditions
- Age limit and relaxation
- Disability certificate requirements
- Application fee
- Required documents
- Posting conditions

## How to apply

Applications must be submitted online through the official **NIC Recruitment Portal**.

Candidates should complete registration, fill the application form, upload the required documents, pay the fee if applicable, and save the final confirmation page.
