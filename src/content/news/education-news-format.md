The Education section is designed for information such as admissions, scholarships, examination notices, results, courses and learning opportunities.

## One consistent article style

Instead of creating a different design for every type of education update, the content collection gives every story a consistent headline, summary, date, image and article body.

That consistency makes it easier for readers to recognise and browse education news.
