The **Department of Posts, Government of India** has opened **Gramin Dak Sevak (GDS) Online Engagement Schedule-II, July-2026**.

This is an important recruitment update for Deaf and Hard of Hearing candidates. In the Department of Posts' official 2026 GDS disability-suitability table, **Deaf (D) and Hard of Hearing (HH)** are listed as suitable for **Branch Postmaster (BPM), Assistant Branch Postmaster (ABPM) and Dak Sevak** posts, with **1% reservation** for the Deaf/HH PwBD category.

## Deaf and Hard of Hearing eligibility

The Department of Posts' official 2026 GDS notification identifies the following disability category as suitable for GDS posts:

- **D - Deaf**
- **HH - Hard of Hearing**

The suitability table covers **BPM, ABPM and Dak Sevak**, and lists **1% reservation** for the Deaf/HH category.

Candidates claiming PwBD reservation should ensure that their disability certificate and benchmark-disability status meet the conditions in the active official notification.

## Educational qualification

Applicants should have a **Secondary School Examination / Class 10 pass certificate** from a recognised board with passing marks in:

- **Mathematics**
- **English**

The applicant should also have studied the **local language** of the postal circle/division at least up to Class 10, as required by India Post.

## Age limit

Under the official 2026 GDS framework:

- **Minimum age:** 18 years
- **Maximum age:** 40 years

PwBD candidates receive upper-age relaxation as applicable under the notification. The 2026 framework provides:

- **PwBD:** 10 years
- **PwBD + OBC:** 13 years
- **PwBD + SC/ST:** 15 years

Candidates should confirm the age calculation date and category conditions in the active Schedule-II notification before applying.

## Salary / TRCA

The official 2026 GDS framework lists the following basic **Time Related Continuity Allowance (TRCA)** slabs:

- **BPM:** ₹12,000 to ₹29,380
- **ABPM / Dak Sevak:** ₹10,000 to ₹24,470

GDS engagement is different from regular Central Government employment. Candidates should read the official service-condition details carefully.

## Selection process

There is **no written examination** under the standard GDS selection process.

Applicants are shortlisted through a **system-generated merit list** based on Class 10 marks or the prescribed conversion of grades/points to marks.

Shortlisted candidates must then complete **physical document verification** and other engagement formalities.

## Vacancy details

India Post publishes GDS vacancies **circle-wise and division-wise** on the official GDS portal.

DeafJobsIndia is not repeating an unverified overall vacancy total here. Candidates should use the official **Circlewise Posts Notified / Vacant Posts** section to check the exact posts available in their preferred postal circle and division.

## Important dates

- **Registration:** 31 August to 19 September 2026, up to 5:00 PM
- **Application submission:** 2 September to 21 September 2026, up to 5:00 PM
- **Edit / correction window:** 23 September to 24 September 2026, up to 5:00 PM

India Post states that candidates applying under **Schedule-II, July-2026 must register afresh**. Registration numbers from previous schedules are not valid for this schedule.

## How to apply

1. Open the official **India Post GDS Online Engagement** portal.
2. Complete fresh registration for Schedule-II, July-2026.
3. Select the postal circle/division and check the eligible vacant posts shown for your category and PwBD status.
4. Fill in the application carefully and upload the required documents.
5. Submit the application before **21 September 2026 at 5:00 PM**.
6. Use the correction window on **23-24 September 2026** if required.

## Important note for Deaf candidates

DeafJobsIndia has verified that the Department of Posts' official 2026 GDS framework lists **Deaf and Hard of Hearing candidates as suitable for BPM, ABPM and Dak Sevak posts with 1% reservation in the D/HH category**.

However, candidates should always read the **active Schedule-II notification**, check the exact circle/division vacancy, and verify the category shown against the post before submitting an application.
