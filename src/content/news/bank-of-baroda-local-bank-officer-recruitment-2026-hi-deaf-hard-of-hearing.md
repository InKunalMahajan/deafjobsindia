**Bank of Baroda** has announced recruitment for **2,482 Local Bank Officer (LBO)** posts on a regular basis under Advertisement No. **BOB/HRM/REC/ADVT/2026/16**.

The recruitment is **Pan India** and includes a separate **HI (Hearing Impaired)** category under PwBD horizontal reservation. The vacancy table shows **37 HI vacancies**, making this recruitment relevant for eligible Deaf and Hard of Hearing candidates.

## Quick recruitment details

| Detail | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Organization | Bank of Baroda |
| Post | Local Bank Officer (LBO), JMG Scale-I |
| Advertisement | BOB/HRM/REC/ADVT/2026/16 |
| Total vacancies | 2,482 |
| HI vacancies | **37** |
| Location | Pan India |
| Application start | 18 August 2026 |
| Last date | **7 September 2026** |
| Application mode | Online |

## 37 HI vacancies for Deaf and Hard of Hearing candidates

The recruitment vacancy table includes **37 posts under the HI category**. PwBD reservation is horizontal, so candidates should check the state-wise vacancy table and all eligibility conditions before applying.

For Deaf and Hard of Hearing candidates, the important point is that the notification does not only mention PwBD generally — it includes a specific **HI vacancy column**.

## State-wise recruitment

The 2,482 vacancies are distributed across multiple states. Candidates apply against a state and must meet the required **local language** condition for that state.

Some of the larger state totals include:

- **Gujarat:** 1,185 vacancies
- **Karnataka:** 418 vacancies
- **Maharashtra:** 218 vacancies
- **Tamil Nadu:** 132 vacancies
- **Odisha:** 100 vacancies
- **Andhra Pradesh:** 80 vacancies

Candidates should use the official advertisement to check the complete state-wise vacancy and PwBD table.

## Eligibility overview

Candidates should read the detailed advertisement carefully for the final eligibility rules. The recruitment requires conditions relating to:

- Educational qualification
- Relevant banking experience
- Age limit and relaxation
- Local language proficiency
- PwBD disability certification
- State selected in the application

Meeting the HI category alone does not automatically make a candidate eligible; all other post requirements must also be satisfied.

## Selection process

The selection process includes stages specified by Bank of Baroda in the detailed advertisement. Candidates should check the official notification for the latest information about the online test, local language proficiency requirements and interview/selection process.

## Application dates

- **Online application begins:** 18 August 2026
- **Last date to apply and pay the fee:** **7 September 2026**

Applicants should avoid waiting until the last day because the recruitment portal may receive heavy traffic close to the deadline.

## Important note for Deaf candidates

There are **37 HI vacancies across the recruitment**, but they are not necessarily available equally in every state. Candidates should confirm the exact **state-wise HI vacancy** before selecting a state and submitting the application.

Also check the official notification for PwBD documentation, disability percentage requirements, examination accommodations and any instructions applicable to Deaf and Hard of Hearing candidates.
