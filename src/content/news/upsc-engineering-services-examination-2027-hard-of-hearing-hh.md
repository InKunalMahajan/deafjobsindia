**Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)** has announced the **Engineering Services Examination (ESE) 2027**, Examination Notice **No. 02/2027-ENGG.**

This recruitment is important for the Hard of Hearing community because the notified PwBD vacancy distribution includes **6 vacancies for Hard of Hearing (HH) candidates**.

This is not based only on a general PwBD statement. However, **HH suitability differs by engineering service/post**, so candidates must check Annexure-I of the official notification before choosing service preferences.

## Hard of Hearing vacancies confirmed

The ESE 2027 vacancy information provides approximately **480 vacancies**, including **19 vacancies reserved for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD)**.

The PwBD distribution is:

- Locomotor Disability: 11 vacancies
- **Hard of Hearing (HH): 6 vacancies**
- Low Vision: 1 vacancy
- Specific Learning Disability / Multiple Disabilities: 1 vacancy

Therefore, DeafJobsIndia classifies ESE 2027 as a **confirmed Hard of Hearing recruitment opportunity**.

## Important: HH eligibility depends on the service/post

The Engineering Services Examination recruits candidates to several Group A and Group B engineering services and posts.

A candidate should **not assume that HH is suitable for every ESE post**.

UPSC provides a service-wise list of identified PwBD categories and functional requirements in **Annexure-I**. Hard of Hearing candidates should verify that the specific service/post they want is identified as suitable for HH before applying or submitting service preferences.

## Engineering disciplines

ESE 2027 covers four engineering categories:

- Civil Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering
- Electrical Engineering
- Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering

## Educational qualification

Candidates generally need an **Engineering degree or another qualification accepted under the ESE rules**.

Some specified services may accept additional equivalent qualifications. Candidates should read the official notification carefully for their engineering discipline and post.

## Age limit

Candidates generally must be **21 to 30 years of age as on 1 January 2027**, subject to the relaxations and conditions stated in the official notification.

Eligible PwBD candidates may receive age relaxation according to Government of India rules.

## Application fee

The examination fee is **₹200** for candidates required to pay the fee.

**PwBD candidates are exempt from the application fee**, subject to the conditions in the notification.

## Selection process

UPSC ESE 2027 has three stages:

1. Engineering Services Preliminary Examination
2. Engineering Services Main Examination
3. Personality Test / Interview

The **Preliminary Examination is scheduled for 31 January 2027**.

## How to apply

Candidates must apply through the **official UPSC online application portal**.

Before applying, keep the required educational, identity, photograph, signature and PwBD documents ready.

Hard of Hearing candidates claiming PwBD benefits should ensure that they hold the required **benchmark disability certificate** in the prescribed format and satisfy the suitability requirements for the relevant service/post.

## Important dates

- **Notification / applications open:** 16 September 2026
- **Last date to apply:** 6 October 2026
- **Preliminary Examination:** 31 January 2027

## Important note for Hard of Hearing candidates

The recruitment includes **6 HH vacancies**, but this does **not mean every engineering service is open to HH candidates**.

Before applying, candidates should check:

- Annexure-I service/post suitability
- Engineering qualification
- Age and relaxation rules
- Benchmark disability requirements
- Functional classification and physical requirements
- Medical standards

Always rely on the latest official UPSC notice and any subsequent corrigendum or addendum.
