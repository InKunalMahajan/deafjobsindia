**Maharashtra Metro Rail Corporation Limited (MAHA-Metro)** has announced recruitment under **Advertisement No. MAHA-Metro/N/HR/05/2026**, dated **10 August 2026**, for experienced candidates for the **Nagpur Metro Rail Project** and **Pune Metro Rail Project**.

The notification includes **56 contract vacancies** in total and is especially relevant for the Deaf and Hard of Hearing community because MAHA-Metro explicitly identifies **HI (D/HH)** or **HH** as eligible disability categories for selected PwBD posts.

## Deaf / Hard of Hearing eligibility

The official notification identifies the following hearing-disability categories for selected PwBD posts:

### Office Assistant — Nagpur and Pune

For **Office Assistant (Admin)**, the identified PwBD categories include:

- **HI (D/HH)** — Hearing Impaired, Deaf and Hard of Hearing
- VI (B/LV)
- Locomotor Disability categories listed in the notification
- ASD (Mild)
- Multiple Disability involving the listed categories

This means **Deaf (D)** and **Hard of Hearing (HH)** candidates are explicitly included for the Office Assistant post, subject to the other eligibility conditions.

### Technician (Electrical) — Nagpur

The identified PwBD categories include:

- **HH — Hard of Hearing**
- OL — One Leg

### Attendant — Nagpur

The identified PwBD categories include:

- LV — Low Vision
- **HH — Hard of Hearing**
- OL — One Leg

## PwBD vacancy position

The recruitment contains **5 PwBD-reserved seats across posts where hearing-disability categories are identified as eligible**:

| Project | Post | Total Posts | PwBD Seats | Hearing category identified |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Nagpur | Office Assistant | 8 | 2 | HI (D/HH) |
| Nagpur | Technician (Electrical) | 8 | 1 | HH |
| Nagpur | Attendant | 8 | 1 | HH |
| Pune | Office Assistant | 7 | 1 | HI (D/HH) |

**Important:** These 5 PwBD seats are **not stated as exclusively reserved for Deaf/HH candidates**. For Office Assistant, Technician (Electrical) and Attendant, MAHA-Metro lists more than one eligible disability category. DeafJobsIndia therefore does not describe these as “5 Deaf/HH vacancies.”

## Main posts in the recruitment

The notification includes posts such as:

- Junior Engineer (Civil)
- Junior Engineer (Electrical)
- Junior Engineer (Rolling Stock)
- Junior Engineer (S&T)
- Senior Office Assistant (PR/Library)
- Office Assistant
- Technician (Civil)
- Technician (Electrical)
- Technician (RS/Diesel Mechanic)
- Technician (S&T/Electronic Mechanic)
- Multi Tasking Staff cum Motor Driver
- Attendant

Not every post is identified as suitable for Deaf/HH candidates. Applicants should apply only where their disability category and other eligibility conditions match the official notification.

## Qualification for relevant PwBD posts

### Office Assistant

- Full-time graduate in any discipline from a Government-recognized institute/university.
- Minimum **5 years of relevant experience** in office/corporate work such as document control, reception, letter drafting, HR or accounts-related activities, along with the pay-scale/CTC conditions in the notification.
- Maximum age: **45 years**.

### Technician (Electrical) — Nagpur

- ITI (NCVT/SCVT) in **Electrician** trade from a Government-recognized institute.
- Minimum **3 years of relevant experience** in the Electrical Engineering Department, together with the prescribed pay-scale/CTC conditions.
- Maximum age: **40 years**.

### Attendant — Nagpur

- Minimum **HSC pass** from a recognized Board.
- Minimum **5 years of experience** in multi-tasking office work, file handling, dispatch work etc., together with the prescribed pay-scale/CTC conditions.
- Maximum age: **45 years**.

## Maharashtra domicile required

The notification states that the applicant **should be a resident of Maharashtra State and must possess a domicile certificate issued by the competent authority**.

PwBD applicants must submit a disability certificate showing **minimum 40% disability**, issued by the competent authority.

## Application fee

According to the official notification:

- **SC / ST / PwBD / Women:** ₹100 processing charge
- **UR / OBC, including Ex-servicemen:** ₹400

Candidates applying for more than one post must submit a separate application and applicable fee for each post.

## Selection process

The selection process includes:

1. Written Test — 70 marks
2. Interview — 30 marks for candidates qualifying as per merit
3. For MTS / Attendant, an Oral Aptitude Test may be conducted instead of an interview
4. Pre-employment Medical Examination
5. Call letter for candidates found medically fit

## How to apply

Applications must be submitted in the prescribed format with the required documents and two recent passport-size photographs.

The completed application should reach MAHA-Metro by **25 September 2026** through **Speed Post** at:

**General Manager (HR)**  
Maharashtra Metro Rail Corporation Ltd.  
Metro Bhawan, Near Dikshabhoomi,  
Nagpur – 440 010

The envelope should be properly sealed and superscribed with the **Post Name and Advertisement Number**.

## Before applying

Deaf and Hard of Hearing candidates should carefully verify:

- Whether their exact disability category is identified for the chosen post
- Minimum 40% benchmark disability certificate requirement
- Maharashtra domicile requirement
- Qualification and experience criteria
- Prescribed pay-scale/CTC experience conditions
- Age limit and applicable relaxation
- Medical standards
- Application fee and supporting documents
