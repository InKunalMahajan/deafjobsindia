This demo story shows how DeafJobsIndia can organise government scheme information without turning the whole website into a directory.

## Useful details to include

Future stories can highlight the name of the scheme, responsible department, eligibility, benefits, important dates, required documents and official source.

The article page can then provide a fuller explanation for readers who want more detail.
